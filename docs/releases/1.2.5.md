# CountAway 1.2.5

**Time, a little clearer.**

Event times now follow your device's 12- or 24-hour clock setting across Home, the editor, widgets, and sharing.

## What's new

- **Clearer recurrence:** the editor follows the next occurrence of repeating events and describes reminders more accurately.
- **Easier live-hours widgets:** style names are readable in English, Spanish, and Catalan, and timed events appear in a more useful order.
- **Safer setup:** the selected event stays selected when the screen is recreated, and events without a time cannot be saved to a live-hours widget.

## Get CountAway

- **GitHub:** download the signed APK attached to this release once published.
- **Google Play:** [CountAway on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.santiagorodriguez.countaway). Updates may arrive separately.
