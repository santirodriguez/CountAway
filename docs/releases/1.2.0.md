# CountAway 1.2.0

Less widget fiddling. More looking forward to things.

## What's new

- **Widgets, with fewer taps:** long-press a countdown, choose from nine styles, preview it, and add it to your Home screen on supported launchers.
- **Dates that come around again:** weekly and monthly repeats join yearly ones, with reminders that follow along and sensible handling of shorter months.
- **Share the anticipation:** send a Ridge-style countdown card in light or dark mode, in your chosen language. Long titles, emoji, and line breaks get along better now.

## A little less friction

Edits and backup steps hold their place when the screen rotates or you leave and return. Widget setup handles longer lists and larger text better, previews stay fresh, and daily counts advance without reopening the app. Reminder timing also gets a few fixes.

Classic, Mist, Horizon, and Sunset get a visual touch-up; Help gets a tidy-up. English, Spanish, and Catalan stay available offline.

## Your countdowns come with you

Existing events, reminders, widgets, and older backups remain readable. Everything still stays on your device: no accounts, ads, analytics, or Internet permission.

For the release notes collectors: versionCode 9, Android 8+ (API26), target/compile SDK36, schema6 for recurrence. No new runtime dependencies or permissions. Older app versions can recognize newer backups without mistaking them for damaged files.
