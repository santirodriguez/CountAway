# CountAway 1.1.7

CountAway 1.1.7 keeps counting after zero and stops offering reminders that would require time travel.

## New

- Past countdowns now show how many days ago they happened in the app, fixed widgets, and native sharing.

## Improved

- Compact widgets keep past events visually distinct from upcoming ones.
- Reminder choices avoid schedules that have already passed.
- Existing historical reminder settings remain untouched unless you change them.

No new permissions, accounts, network access, analytics, or dependencies.
